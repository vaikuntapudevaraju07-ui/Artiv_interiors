## Brand & Style

This design system expresses quiet architectural luxury, residential bespoke craftsmanship, and timeless hospitality. Designed for an elite bespoke interior studio, the aesthetic bridges heritage detailing with contemporary restraint. The visual language evokes feelings of calm distinction, tactile richness, and deliberate pacing—mirroring the experience of walking through an expertly curated private estate.

The stylistic foundation fuses **Minimalism** with an **Editorial Architectural** sensibility. Spacious layouts, generous breathing margins, refined typography, and organic tonality replace aggressive calls-to-action with thoughtful, gallery-like presentations. Imagery of curated spaces, millwork, and stone textures are elevated via understated framing, delicate hair-thin dividers, and warm ambient surface layering.

## Colors

The palette revolves around organic, mineral-derived tones reminiscent of limewash plaster, raw silk, polished brass, and aged teak. 

- **Primary (`#8C7B6D`)**: Deep taupe provides foundational gravitas for structural accents, secondary interactive states, and active tags without the harshness of pure black.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059`)**: Muted satin gold, applied selectively for focal highlights, bespoke badges, key interactive states, and artisanal details.
- **Neutral (`#FAF8F5`)**: Natural warm white evoking tactile handmade paper and sunlit lime plaster, anchoring the primary canvas.
- **Named Surfaces & Charcoal Tones**:
  - `surface-cream` (`#F5F1EB`) & `surface-sand` (`#E6DFD5`) form natural stepped elevation tiers for cards, side panels, and inset galleries.
  - `text-primary` (`#222222`) ensures uncompromising editorial contrast for headlines and editorial titles, softened by `charcoal-soft` (`#333333`) and `text-muted` (`#635D57`) for long-form narrative body and captions.
  - `border-hairline` (`#E2DACF`) preserves structural clarity with zero visual heaviness.

## Typography

The typography pairs the high-fashion, classical refinement of **Playfair Display** with the pristine geometric warmth of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`)**: Set with generous vertical breathing room and subtle negative tracking on large scales. It commands architectural dignity and an editorial mood across heroes, project titles, and thematic section openers.
- **Body & Captions (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Neutral, open, and effortless to read at all sizes. Retains exceptional legibility across dense architectural spec sheets, project narratives, and interior floor plans.
- **Labels & Overlines**: Rendered in uppercase `Plus Jakarta Sans` with expanded letter-spacing (0.08em–0.12em) to impart a refined gallery plaque aesthetic.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an asymmetric, editorial 12-column grid that allows architectural imagery and descriptive narrative to breathe unhurriedly. 

- **Desktop (1024px and above)**: 12-column grid, 4rem canvas margins, 1.5rem gutters. Max content bounding box: 1400px. Asymmetric alignments (e.g., 5-column editorial text paired against 7-column hero photography) convey high-end magazine art direction.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column grid, 2rem margins, 1.25rem gutters. Sections gracefully collapse into balanced two-tier compositions.
- **Mobile (below 768px)**: 4-column grid, 1.25rem margins, 1rem gutters. Hero imagery transitions from wide horizontal formats to commanding 4:5 vertical frames.
- **Rhythm**: Generous vertical spacing (`space-xl` or larger) between thematic sections prevents visual clutter and fosters an atmosphere of unhurried sophistication.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy avoids generic synthetic drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layering** and **Soft Amber-Tinted Ambient Diffusions**.

- **Surface Tiers**: Base depth is established through tonal shift. The base canvas (`#FAF8F5`) holds layered panels and cards in `#F5F1EB` or `#E6DFD5`, demarcated with gentle `1px solid #E2DACF` hairline borders rather than heavy drop shadows.
- **Ambient Shadow Character**: When cards, elevated consultation modals, or interactive portfolios lift off the canvas, they use multi-layered diffused shadows tinted with warm taupe rather than neutral grey:
  - *Resting Card Elevation*: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(140, 123, 109, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(34, 34, 34, 0.03)`
  - *Hover / Lifted Elevation*: `0 16px 32px -8px rgba(140, 123, 109, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.08)`
  - *Floating Dialogues / Menus*: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(34, 34, 34, 0.14)`

## Shapes

The design system utilizes moderate, refined corner radii (`roundedness: 2`, corresponding to `0.5rem` / `8px` for standard elements, `1rem` / `16px` for portfolio and showcase cards, and `1.5rem` / `24px` for modal sheets). 

This balanced curve softens rectilinear architectural lines and masonry structures without falling into whimsical or overly playful circularity. Image containers, interior swatch cards, and interactive buttons share this disciplined geometric curvature, pairing seamlessly with hairline borders to emulate bespoke joinery and hand-carved finishes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Rich dark charcoal background (`#222222`), pure off-white text (`#FAF8F5`), 8px border radius. Padding: `0.875rem 1.75rem`. Hover triggers a smooth 200ms transition to primary taupe (`#8C7B6D`) or deep satin gold border glow.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Hairline border (`1px solid #8C7B6D`), transparent background, charcoal text (`#222222`). Hover transitions to cream surface fill (`#F5F1EB`).
- **Accent (Bespoke Inquiries)**: Satin gold background (`#C5A059`), deep charcoal text (`#222222`), font-weight 600.

### Cards & Project Galleries
- Built on `surface-cream` (`#F5F1EB`) with a subtle `1px solid #E2DACF` border and 16px radius (`rounded-lg`).
- Editorial metadata (project square footage, location, materials) is presented in `label-md` tracking above the title.
- Image containers feature fixed architectural aspect ratios (4:3, 16:9, or 4:5 portrait) with subtle zoom transforms (`scale(1.02)`) on parent card hover.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background: `#FAF8F5` (or pure `#FFFFFF` against `#F5F1EB` card containers).
- Border: `1px solid #E2DACF`, 8px corner radius.
- Focus State: Replaces browser defaults with a defined `1px solid #C5A059` border and an ambient `0 0 0 3px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.15)` focus ring.
- Placeholder text uses `text-muted` (`#635D57`) with comfortable interior padding (`0.875rem 1rem`).

### Chips & Material Tags
- Architectural material filters (e.g., "Italian Marble", "Teak Wood", "Brass Accents") use compact pill shapes (`rounded-xl` or fully rounded).
- Unselected: Inset surface sand (`#E6DFD5`), charcoal text (`#333333`), zero border.
- Selected: Charcoal background (`#222222`), off-white text (`#FAF8F5`), accompanied by a subtle gold indicator pip.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- 20px custom form controls with 4px border radius (checkbox) or circular contour (radio).
- Border: `1.5px solid #8C7B6D`.
- Checked State: Filled with `#C5A059` accented by an off-white checkmark or central dot.

### Specialized Architectural Components
- **Material Swatch Drawer**: Horizontal scrollable container exhibiting finishes (fabrics, stone samples, metal hardware) with tactile zoom preview on selection.
- **Before / After Split Canvas**: Smooth slider with a satin-gold vertical dividing hairline and polished circular drag node.
